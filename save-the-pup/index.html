Privacy Policy for Save the Pup

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Who we are

Save the Pup is a mobile game published by Atlas Forge Studios. For any question about this policy or your data, write to contact@atlasforge-studios.com.

What the game stores

The game does not ask you to create an account and does not send us any data. Your progress (stars earned, levels unlocked) and your settings (sound) are stored on your phone only. They are deleted if you uninstall the game.

Ads

The game is free and funded by video ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited in Europe). To show these ads, measure how they perform and fight fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • technical information about your device (model, operating system, language);
  • your interactions with the ads (views, clicks, videos watched).

Google processes this data under its own rules: how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can accept, refuse or choose in detail. If you refuse, non-personalised ads may still be shown.

You can change your mind at any time with the "Privacy" button in the game menu. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings (Settings › Google › Ads).

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, and object to its processing. Since the game sends us no data, these requests mostly concern Google, which you can contact from the page above. You can also write to us at contact@atlasforge-studios.com, or contact the data protection authority of your country.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, apart from the ads described above. If you believe a child has sent us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.
