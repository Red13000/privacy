Pin Pop! Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Who we are

Pin Pop! is a mobile puzzle game published by Atlas Forge Studios. For any question about this policy or your data, write to contact@atlasforge-studios.com.

What the game stores

The game does not ask you to create an account and does not send us any data. Your progress (stars earned, levels unlocked, skipped levels, a bonus hint waiting for the next level) and your settings (sound) are stored on your phone only. They are deleted if you uninstall the game.

Advertising

The game is free and funded by video ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited in Europe). Some videos play between levels; others are optional and give a reward (a hint, skipping a level). To show these ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • technical information about the device (model, operating system, language);
  • your interactions with ads (views, clicks, videos watched).

Google processes this data under its own rules: how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can accept, refuse or choose in detail. If you refuse, non-personalised ads may still be shown.

You can change your mind at any time with the “Privacy” button in the game menu. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your phone).

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, and object to its processing. Since the game sends us no data, these requests mainly concern Google, which you can contact from the page above. You can also write to us at contact@atlasforge-studios.com, or contact the data protection authority in your country.

Children

Pin Pop! is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, apart from the advertising described above. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.
